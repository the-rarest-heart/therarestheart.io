Meet Gerdie

He's a guide, not a mascot.

Gerdie turns up when a term needs translating, when a page is empty, or when you're one click from giving up. He goes quiet on anything serious — a breach, a legal line, real risk. A cartoon being cute about those is how you lose trust.

He's docked bottom-right on every page. He never opens himself.
