Read this part carefully

You're right to be suspicious of someone asking for remote access.

That's exactly the scam I'm protecting your parent from. A stranger calls, says there's a virus, talks them into handing over their screen, and empties an account. If you weren't a little suspicious of me right now, I'd be worried about you.

So here's what I will never do. In writing, on the page, before you give me a dollar.

  • ✓I will never call your parent out of the blue. Real help starts with you contacting me. If someone calls them claiming to be me, it isn't me.
  • ✓I will never ask for or type a password. If one's needed, they type it while I look away. I don't write passwords down and I don't save them anywhere.
  • ✓I will never ask to be paid by gift card, wire transfer, crypto, or a cash app. I send an invoice. Any other payment request is not from me.
  • ✓I will never install anything that lets me reconnect later. Your parent reads me a one-time code to start the session. When it ends, my access ends.
  • ✓I will never work on a screen your parent isn't watching. They see everything I do, as I do it, and they can stop me at any second without explaining why.
  • ✓I will never move money, open an account, or buy anything on their behalf.
  • ✓I will never touch files, photos, or email beyond what we agreed to.

Every client signs a consent form with all of this on it, before the first session.

Questions

The things families ask me first.

What if my parent isn't comfortable with a screen share?
Then we start on the phone. Plenty of what I do — spotting scam texts, checking whether an email is real, walking through a setting — works fine by voice. The screen share is a convenience, not a requirement.
What if they live somewhere else?
Doesn't matter. That's the point of doing this remotely. If you're in Denver and they're in Charleston, nobody drives.
Can I be on the call too?
Please do. A lot of families prefer it, especially the first time.
What if it turns out to need someone physically there?
I'll tell you straight. Some things — a dead hard drive, bad cabling, a router that needs replacing — can't be done over a screen. I'll say so, tell you what it needs, and I won't bill you for the session that found out.
What if my parent has memory problems?
Then I need you to sign the consent form too, and I'd want you on the first call. I'm not comfortable relying on verbal permission from someone who may not remember giving it, and you shouldn't be either.
Do you keep any access afterward?
No. Nothing installed, nothing running, no standing access. Every session starts with a fresh code they give me.
How do I pay?
Card, after the session, by invoice. Never a gift card, never a wire, never a cash app — see above.
